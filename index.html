Questions

Is my statement uploaded?

No. The page has no server. You can open your browser's network inspector while converting: no request carries your file. It also works offline once loaded.

Which banks are supported?

Any statement with selectable text and a date at the start of each transaction line: checking, savings and credit‑card statements in US (MM/DD) and European (DD/MM, DD Mon) date formats, with single Amount columns or separate Debit / Credit columns. Scanned or photographed statements are not supported.

How accurate is it?

When a running balance column exists, every amount is cross‑checked against it and signs are corrected automatically. Always spot‑check totals before filing anything. The free first page lets you verify quality on your own bank before paying.
